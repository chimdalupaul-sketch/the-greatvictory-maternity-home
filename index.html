ABOUT US

A welcoming place for mothers and children

The GreatVictory Maternity Home is located beside Astana Table Water in Akaboukwu, Uruagu, Nnewi, Anambra State, Nigeria.

The our medical Health Center is at Back to land filling station in Akaboukwu, Uruagu, Nnewi, Anambra State, Nigeria.

We aim to provide a welcoming environment for mothers and child seeking maternity care and related information.
